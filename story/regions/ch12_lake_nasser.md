## ARRIVAL
Nour sails you south, and **Hajja Fatma** insists on coming: *"I want to see the water that
sits on my mother's house."* The emotional heart of Act III is here. It's quiet, sad and
beautiful.

---

## MAIN BEATS

1. **Qasr Ibrim.** The fortress on its hilltop-turned-island, still where it's always been.
   Hajja Fatma tells the story of the 1960s: the relocation, the villages drowned, and the
   temples saved while the people's homes weren't (real). A Keeper mark on the fortress
   wall points to **Old Ballana**.
2. **Uncle Idris's camp.** A lake fishing camp that serves as a rest point. Idris knows where
   Old Ballana lies under the water, because his nets catch on its palm stumps.
3. **The dive to Old Ballana.** A whole drowned Nubian village: domed mud-brick houses, a
   painted doorway still visible, a school, a mosque's base, palm stumps.
   - **Hajja Fatma's family house:** her mother's key (from SQ-11-02) opens a tin chest
     (it's miraculously dry, sealed in tar) holding family photos. This is the most
     emotional find in the game.
   - **The village's ancient shrine** (a Meroitic-era chapel the village was built around):
     **House 6b**, with **60 scrolls** of Nubian and Kushite royal annals, the history of
     the Black Pharaohs from their own side.
   - **The choice** → `house_6b` / `ch12_left_in_place`:
     - **Raise the scrolls:** a Library completeness bonus for ending variations.
     - **Leave them where they are**, with Hajja Fatma's blessing: Nubian Sworn, and an
       epilogue line: *"Some things belong to the water now, and to us."*
4. **Record the memories** (`ch12_memory`). Hajja Fatma sits on the boat's deck and tells
   the whole story of Old Ballana while the sun sets. Recording it on your phone sets the
   flag for the Return and Discovery epilogues (the village restored in a museum).
5. **Abu Simbel.** You fly out or land here. If it's festival time (the in-game date is
   near the Sun Festival, or you can wait for it), watch the sunrise light the inner
   sanctuary (real). Vasse's helicopter lands at the airport. Lena is on the tarmac. It's a
   tense meeting, not a fight: she says, *"Sinai. He's going to Sinai. Choose soon."*
   `rel_lena` ≥ 30 gives a precise warning, and below that she says nothing useful.
6. **The Claimed, stage 3 begins:** they go quiet and stare at the water.

**Leaving:** a flight from Abu Simbel (real airport), then Cairo, then **Ch13 Sinai**.

## JOBS
- Lake fishing (Nile perch and tilapia)
- Diving salvage (the drowned villages: legal, with a permit)
- Guiding (Abu Simbel)

## SECRETS
- **The drowned school:** a child's slate with a Nobiin alphabet. It's a collectible, and
  it teaches Nubian +1
- **The Qasr Ibrim cistern:** a Meroitic treasure (Major)
- **Keeper shrine tile #10**, carved on a lake island rock

## DAY / NIGHT
- The lake is calm at dawn and windy in the afternoon.
- Crocodiles bask on the shore at midday (a hazard).
