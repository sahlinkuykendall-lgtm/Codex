## ARRIVAL
The cheapest and quietest region in the game, and one of the most beautiful. **Dr. Kamal
Aziz** (the Minya inspector) is delighted anyone came. He's honest, broke and hungry for
help. Under the giant baboons of Thoth at el-Ashmunein, the player feels for the first
time that they're standing in **Thoth's own city**.

---

## MAIN BEATS

1. **Thoth's city.** At el-Ashmunein, Kamal gives a real history walk: Khemenu, the Eight
   Gods, and Thoth as the god of writing, magic and the moon. The Codex's next entry names
   *"the house of the ibis, beside the priest who wrote in two tongues"*. That's **Petosiris**,
   whose tomb mixes Greek and Egyptian art (real).
2. **Tuna el-Gebel.** You visit the tomb of Petosiris by day. By night (a stealth or
   Ministry permit) you go into the **ibis galleries**: kilometers of tunnels, millions of
   mummified ibises in pots (real), and baboon burials.
3. **The warning.** At a sealed gallery mouth, a single line is carved in the almost-
   hieroglyphic script, with a Coptic gloss beside it: *"Do not read this aloud."*
   **Miriam read it aloud.** A fresh chalk mark beside it is her initials.
   - **Deniable moment (sealed-track safe):** ibises in the cliff roost all turn to face you
     at once. Kamal laughs: *"They do that when a car goes by."* No car went by.
4. **Finding Miriam.** Deeper in, a **Keeper hall** is set up in a side gallery: lamps,
   cots, prayer mats. **Miriam Hale** is there, gaunt, talking softly to an ibis in a cage.
   Then **Sitt Meret** appears in person for the first time. She's gentle and immovable:
   *"She stays with us until she's well, my child. Or until you give us a reason."*
   **`ch8_miriam`** ★:
   - **Negotiate** (needs Keepers ≥ Neutral): give your word you'll consult Sitt Meret
     before opening the last door. Miriam comes with you *in a week*, and `miriam_state`
     stays open. Keepers +10.
   - **Sneak her out** at night: Keepers -20, and Miriam joins you now (as an ally).
   - **Let her stay:** she re-enters in Ch13 (and is safer from Vasse).
5. **What's in the box.** Miriam (or Sitt Meret, if Miriam stays) tells the **Setne Khamwas
   tale**: Naneferkaptah, the Book, the river that took his wife and son, and Setne who
   stole it and gave it back. Then Petamun's third letter makes it plain: *"The box we
   carried from the burning house is the Book of Thoth, or what the priests of Khemenu
   believed it to be. I have not opened it. I have given it the deepest house but two."*
6. **House 4.** Speak the **Word of Amun** at a seven-tone door deep in the galleries. It's
   the Siwa key, or the Bahariya fallback. Inside: **90 scrolls** of medicine (lost works of
   the Alexandrian anatomists, such as Herophilus, which is real and lost), and the key to
   House 5: *"In the western hills of Thebes, in the tomb the robbers keep."*
   Miriam or Hagg Mahmoud's history gives it away: **the Gebali family's hidden tomb in
   Qurna.**
7. **Vasse's secret** (optional but important) → `ch8_medical_file`. The Foundation paid a
   private Minya clinic to fly in a specialist. Break into its records: **Vasse is dying.**
   This is a Radwan proof item. For the Journalist it becomes a published story (Vasse -20).

**Leaving:** south to **Ch9 Luxor** by train, road, or the first river boat.

## SECRETS
- **The baboon catacomb's golden baboon** (Major)
- **Amarna's hidden sculptor's workshop:** a second, unfinished Nefertiti bust fragment (the
  real one was found at Amarna in 1912)
- **Keeper shrine tile #6** in the tomb of Petosiris's forecourt

## DAY / NIGHT
- The galleries are gated by day, so night entry is required.
- The ibis roost flies at dawn: a staged spectacle every morning.
