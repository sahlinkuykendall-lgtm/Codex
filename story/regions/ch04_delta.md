## ARRIVAL
A microbus through green fields. The Delta is the opposite of every desert cliché. At San
el-Hagar, the ruin-field is a sea of broken granite. **Mahrous**, the site guard, asks what
you want, and knows exactly what a bribe is.

**Abu Ali** fishes the lagoon behind the site. He becomes your shelter.

---

## MAIN BEATS

1. **Reading the star disk.** The disk only works at night, aligned to the real Delta sky.
   You need to be inside the ruin-field after closing: bribe Mahrous, get Amira's night
   permit (Ministry Warm), or sneak in.
2. **The alignment puzzle.** Set the disk on a fallen obelisk's base and turn it to match
   the stars (a star-alignment puzzle). The line it throws points at the temple of Mut's
   dry **sacred lake**.
3. **The Radwan checkpoint** (the staged event). Abu Ali warns: *"Police set a checkpoint
   on the bridge at sunset."* At sunset, **Colonel Radwan** himself is at the checkpoint,
   waiting for you. The standoff → `ch4_radwan` ★:
   - **Spare** him (walk away when you could have humiliated him): conscience +1.
   - **Expose** him on camera (with Journalist or photo evidence): `radwan_path` = corrupt,
     locked early, and Ministry +15.
   - **Blackmail** him with Omar's debt: he's a reluctant tool.

   Proof items you might have also count toward his conscience: Magdy's photos
   (`ch1d_magdy_paid`), Lindqvist's papers (SQ-01A-03), the Theodora records
   (SQ-03-04). Showing him proof here instead of exposing him is another route to
   conscience +1.
4. **House 2.** Under the sacred lake bed, a shaft goes down into the water table. The Delta
   is wet, and water seeps in everywhere. It's a descent through three flooding chambers
   (water-level puzzles). At the bottom: **70 scrolls**, including the complete
   ***Aegyptiaca* of Manetho** (the real lost history of Egypt, known only in fragments),
   and a **cipher grid** carved in the ceiling.
5. **Everyone arrives.** The pumps are failing and the chamber is flooding. Amira (with
   Radwan's police, unless he was exposed), Lena (Vasse), and Karim (Gebali) come down the
   shaft at once.
   > **BIG CHOICE 1: "WHOSE SIDE ARE YOU ON?"** → `bc1_side`
   > The Ministry, Vasse, the Gebali, or Alone. See `03_CHOICES_AND_FLAGS.md` §3 for all
   > the consequences.
6. **The flood.** The sacred-lake chamber collapses and floods. **Save Mahrous (pinned
   under a fallen block) or the last scroll crate** → `ch4_mahrous` ★.
7. **Escape.** Tanis floods behind you, and the site is closed for years (**one-way**).
   - **Hide in Abu Ali's reeds** overnight (boat stealth through the lagoon) →
     `ch4_reeds`: Abu Ali becomes a river contact in Ch10.
   - **Run the checkpoint** (car chase, Youssef if you have him): heat +2.
8. **The cipher grid** (copied from the ceiling, or photographed) points southwest:
   *"the house of a thousand rooms."* Amira knows it at once: Herodotus's lost
   **Labyrinth of Hawara**, in **the Faiyum**.
