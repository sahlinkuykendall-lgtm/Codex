# ECONOMY

**Model: GTA V.** Money is a side game that runs alongside the story. You earn it through
jobs, side missions and finds, and spend it on things that make you faster, safer and
freer. The main story **never requires grinding**: every story cost has a free, harder
route, and a nearby job can pay for it in about 30–45 minutes.

Currency: **Egyptian pounds (EGP)**. Prices are game-world values, loosely based on the
2020s, and tuned for fun rather than exact accuracy.

---

## 1. REGIONAL PRICES (and the in-world reason)

Prices are the base price × the region's multiplier. Every region has one NPC line that
explains its prices, so players understand *why* it costs more there.

| Region | × | Why (and who says it) |
|---|---|---|
| Cairo | 1.0 | Youssef: *"In Cairo there are two prices: the price, and the price for your face. Speak Arabic, and you get the first one."* |
| Alexandria | 1.3 | Yannis: *"Rich port, old money, sea view. You pay for the sea view even when you're facing the wall."* |
| The Delta | 0.8 | Abu Ali: *"Here, the fish is from the lake and the bread is from my wife. What should it cost?"* |
| Faiyum | 0.8 | Sayed: *"No tourists means honest prices. Well, more honest."* |
| Western Desert | 1.6 | Aisha: *"Everything here came 400 kilometers on a truck. Including the water. **Especially** the water."* |
| Siwa | 1.4 | Mona: *"Nine hours from anywhere. The dates are cheap. Nothing else is."* |
| Middle Egypt (Minya) | 0.7 | Dr. Kamal: *"Nobody comes here. It's the cheapest place in Egypt, and the most beautiful. Don't tell anyone."* |
| Luxor | 1.2 | Hassan: *"Three cruise boats a day. Everyone here learned to count in dollars."* |
| The River | 1.0 | — |
| Aswan | 1.1 | — |
| Nubian villages | 0.6 | Nour: *"We trade. Bring something useful and you'll eat like a king."* |
| Lake Nasser | 1.5 | Uncle Idris: *"The nearest shop is a boat ride and a prayer away."* |
| Sinai | 1.5 | Sheikh Mousa: *"Mountain prices. The mountain does not negotiate."* |
| Giza | 1.2 | — |

**The tourist price:** foreign backgrounds pay ×1.5 in markets and taxis until
**Egyptian Arabic ≥ 2**. You can also avoid it by haggling (Haggling skill), or by being
with Youssef, Nour or another local ally. Egyptian backgrounds never pay it.

## 3. PROPERTY (home bases)

A home base gives you:
- Rest and save.
- A stash (unlimited storage).
- A trophy room showing finds and Houses discovered.
- A wardrobe.
- Base upgrades.

| Property | Region | Cost | Upgrades |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Umm Hassan's rooftop room** | Cairo | Rent 1,200/week (or free if SQ-02-04 is done) | Fix the water tank (tea minigame at home), a pigeon coop (message pigeons are a fun collectible), a desk (faster Codex study) |
| **Zamalek flat** | Cairo | 2,500,000 | A Nile view. Guests can visit (romance scenes), a gear locker, a garage |
| **The dahabiya *Hathor*** | Luxor → the whole Nile | Story, Ch9 | A galley (cooking buffs), a crew (Captain Ramzi and a cook), a dive platform, a study, and sails (faster river travel). It sails between Luxor, Edfu, Kom Ombo and Aswan |
| **Siwa mud-brick house** | Siwa | 400,000 | A date garden (passive income 3,000/week) and a spring pool |
| **Nubian guest room** | Aswan | 150,000 (or free at Nubian Sworn) | Painted walls, which change with your Nubian rep |

**Passive income** (checked weekly in-game):
- Siwa dates: 3,000.
- Dahabiya charter (if you let Ramzi rent it out while you're away): 8,000.
- Owned felucca fares: 2,500.
- A Cairo tawla café share (SQ-02-07): 4,000.

## 4. FINDS (selling what you dig up)

There are four find grades. You get them from the detector, sieving, digging, tombs,
diving and rewards.

| Grade | Examples | Market value |
|---|---|---|
| Common | Sherds, beads, Roman coins | 200–2,000 |
| Good | Amulets, shabtis, oil lamps | 3,000–15,000 |
| Rare | A statuette, a coffin fragment, a papyrus scrap | 20,000–120,000 |
| Major | A complete stela, a gold object, a Cambyses sword | 150,000–1,000,000 |

**Where to sell:**

| Buyer | Pays | Consequences |
|---|---|---|
| **The Ministry** (report it) | 20% as a finder's reward | Ministry rep +, no heat. The find appears in a museum (the trophy room shows the museum label) |
| **Gebali fences** (Darb al-Ahmar, Qurna, Aswan) | 70% | Gebali +, Ministry -, **heat +1 for Rare or Major**. Nubian finds anger the Nubian community |
| **Vasse Foundation** | 100% (needs Warm) | Vasse +, Ministry -, Keepers - (if from a House). No heat (Vasse "handles it") |
| **Street buyers** | 40% | Risky: 30% chance it's a police sting (heat +2) |

**Appraisal:** the Excavation or Hieroglyphs skill lets you judge value and **spot fakes**.
Scammers sell fakes in every tourist market (the "Scammed" failure state).

**Story items** (the Codex, House scrolls, the Book) can only be sold through story
choices, never at a shop.

## 6. MONEY PRESSURE (why it's challenging)

Each act has natural costs the player feels:

| Act | Pressure |
|---|---|
| I | The Fixer's 60,000 debt. Coptic lessons (or Wassef's fee). Dive kit for Alexandria. The Tanis bribe (or the reeds route). Weekly rent |
| II | Desert gear and water, and a camel or jeep. Siwa permits and bribes. Luxor West Bank permits (or Gebali favors). Dahabiya upgrades (optional) |
| III | The Nubian tutor. Boat hire. Dive gear for Philae and Lake Nasser. Sinai permits and a jeep. **Ch14 preparation:** buying your allies gear (see Ch14; better-equipped allies survive) |

Plus random costs from failure states: bail, hospital, robbery, fines and impound.

**Debt:** you can borrow from Madame Samira (Cairo) at 20% per in-game week. Unpaid debt
sends debt collectors (a failure state).
